 <p style="text-align: justify;">
  Long-period transiants (LPTs) are a recently identified class of Galactic radio transients with spin periods of several tens of minutes. Their physical nature remains debated: while some systems appear consistent with highly magnetized white dwarfs, several sources show no evidence of binarity and instead display behaviour reminiscent of neutron stars, potentially extending the magnetar family. The discovery of DART/ASKAP J1832–0911 (DA J1832), a ∼44-minute periodic radio and X-ray transient, has intensified interest in this possibility.
  </p>
